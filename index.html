Server Rules

Follow these rules to keep DarknessSMP fun for everyone.

1 No Hacking or Cheating
Using hacked clients, X-ray, or any cheat mods is strictly prohibited and will result in a permanent ban.
2 No Griefing
Destroying or stealing from other players' builds without permission is not allowed.
3 No Spamming or Advertising
Do not spam chat or advertise other servers. This will result in a mute or ban.
4 Respect All Players
Harassment, racism, or any form of toxic behaviour will not be tolerated.
5 No Exploiting Bugs
If you find a bug or exploit, report it to staff. Abusing exploits is bannable.
6 Listen to Staff
Follow instructions from staff members. If you believe a decision was unfair, appeal on Discord.
